FarmLedger is an open-source, AI-powered platform that combines **[Mindledger](https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/MindLedger)** and **[EviAI](https://codeberg.org/RoxanneA/EviAI)** on the back end to provide a **direct-to-consumer e-commerce system** for both **current and future inventory**. # FarmLedger

**Forecast, Track, Deliver**

FarmLedger is an open-source, AI-powered direct-to-consumer commerce platform built for agriculture, food production, and future inventory markets. It combines **MindLedger** and **EviAI** to create a transparent marketplace where producers can sell both **available inventory** and **future production** using secure smart contracts, AI forecasting, and integrated financial management.

Designed for farms, ranches, orchards, fisheries, cooperatives, artisans, and food producers, FarmLedger enables customers to purchase products that are available today or reserve future harvests, livestock, seasonal goods, and production capacity with confidence.

---

# Vision

FarmLedger modernizes agricultural commerce by connecting producers directly with consumers while providing complete transparency throughout the production lifecycle. Through intelligent forecasting, automated contracts, secure payments, and comprehensive inventory management, FarmLedger helps producers reduce uncertainty while giving buyers greater confidence in every purchase.
