# OriginScope

**From Launch to Ledger - Tracked.**

OriginScope is an open-source cryptocurrency intelligence platform that provides complete lifecycle transparency for digital assets. It documents where cryptocurrencies originate, who created them, how they were funded, what technical documentation supports them, who promotes them, and how projects evolve over time.

Rather than functioning as a simple cryptocurrency directory, OriginScope is designed as a modular intelligence platform capable of correlating blockchain projects, founders, organizations, investors, social media activity, and historical events into a searchable knowledge graph.

The system is built around independent modules that can be deployed together or extended through optional plug-ins, allowing organizations and researchers to tailor OriginScope to their specific analytical needs.

---

# Specification

OriginScope provides a modular framework for collecting, validating, analyzing, and visualizing cryptocurrency intelligence.

The platform is designed around several principles:

- Complete cryptocurrency lifecycle tracking
- Founder and organization transparency
- Historical preservation of blockchain projects
- Verifiable documentation and evidence
- Extensible architecture
- Open APIs
- Community-driven data improvements
- Graph-based relationship analysis
- Automated data collection
- Long-term historical archival
